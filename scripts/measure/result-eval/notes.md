# Talk: Why our team stopped estimating in story points

- Story points started as a way to avoid promising dates. They became dates anyway.
- What we measured for six months: cycle time per ticket, not points.
  - median 3.2 days, 85th percentile 9 days
  - points predicted nothing: tickets marked 1 and 8 had the same median
- What we do now
  - split anything we think is over a week
  - forecast with throughput: tickets per week, last 8 weeks
  - a range, never a single date
- What it changed
  - planning meetings went from 2 hours to 30 minutes
  - stakeholders get a range with a confidence, and trust it more
- What we lost: a number that felt like control
- Takeaway: measure the flow, forecast with ranges, stop debating sizes
