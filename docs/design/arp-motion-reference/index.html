Notes · docs/18 §7 and §8

What each scale earns.
What I kept from wieedze, what I dropped.

BlockAlready shipped and defensible on pacing: a heading, then its rows, so each claim lands on its own. Kept as is, plus one rule: labels fade, they do not slide.build
WordEarns its line in two places only. The hero h1, word by word, because it is the first thing read and the delay follows the rendered line. The manifesto, lit line by line by the scroll, because it paces a statement without hiding it. Word by word on every h2 would only look better: h2s arrive by authored line instead. Figures never animate.build two
RouteEarns its line by removing a flash, not by adding a move: today the loader appears without transition even when the chunk is cached. Opacity only, 120 out and 200 in, loader after 150 ms, nothing on back and forward.build
SaffronYour call, and it breaks a written rule: the accent is meant for the primary action, the selected state and your own position. The dive into the core and the dots and discs between scenes spend it as decoration. If you keep them, the rule in rules/ui.md has to say so.to decide
GSAPStill not justified. The dive and the Saffron hand-overs are scrubbed and reversible, which is GSAP's case, but they are pure functions of the deck's own progress, a few lines of canvas each. The hero sequence is three CSS delays.no dependency

Kept from wieedze

  • Native sticky and live geometry. useScrollytelling reads progress from the track's rect every frame. Same as the ARP deck, kept.
  • Hold, then ease through. Its smoothstep warp makes the hand-over start and end softly. Applied to the ARP fade, which was linear.
  • Visible by default. gsap.from everywhere: without JS nothing is hidden. Same rule as Reveal's canAnimate, extended to words.
  • Beats, not a cascade. Entrance orders beats: the title lands first, the eyebrow fades with it without sliding, the rest follows. Kept as the hero order.
  • The answer as one move. Hero.tsx raises lede, tagline and cue as one tween with no stagger. Kept for the lede and the CTA row.
  • Back and forward are instant. SmoothScroll skips its route effect on popstate. Kept for the route scale.

Dropped

  • Lenis. It lags the whole page behind the wheel, everywhere. ARP takes the wheel only inside the deck, one gesture per scene, and leaves native scroll everywhere else.
  • The 0.8 to 1.4 s durations. Right for a portfolio headline you watch, wrong for a page of figures you read. ARP stays at 240 to 380 ms with a 560 ms ceiling.
  • "in public." sliding in from the side. A sideways move reads as a carousel.
  • The scale-back on leaving panels. Scaling mono text by 3 to 4% softens its rendering, and every figure here is mono.
  • Sheets sliding over sheets. A curtain. docs/18 rejects it for routes and the cross-fade already owns the page.
  • The bobbing scroll arrow. Perpetual motion next to text. The rail says where you are instead.
  • The long empty beat between panels. wieedze leaves a real pause with nothing on screen. ARP keeps it to 4% of a band: enough that two headings never sit on top of each other, too short to read as a cut.
