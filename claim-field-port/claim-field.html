What changed, and why

Kept

  • A ring per predicate, innermost first, in the graph's own order. Nothing re-sorts.
  • Filled, hollow, ringed read straight off positionCount and the counter-triple.
  • Real links, absolutely positioned, the whole triple as the accessible name.
  • The slot is reserved. The card is in the DOM at full height before anything is pointed at.
  • Rings turn, slower outward, alternating, and stop under reduced motion.

Changed

  • The loupe stays, tied to the card. It rests at five past on the outer ring and reads what passes under it. Point at any dot, or Tab to it, and it glides there and rides it; let go and it glides home.
  • The glide is eased, about half a second, never a jump. Under reduced motion it moves at once.
  • It tours on its own. With nobody pointing, every 4 s the loupe glides to the next claim in the graph's order. Pointing takes over; letting go resumes from there. No tour under reduced motion.
  • One claim passes under the loupe once a lap; the card always names the nearest dot, so it is never blank.
  • Beyond 36, the rest is grain. Every claim past the drawn ones is a fine stipple on the same ring, so 250 looks dense, not like 36.
  • More than 5 predicates fold into a last ring, "+N other predicates", named one by one in the legend.
  • 28px targets around a 10px mark.

To decide

  • Size by position count. A dot grows a little with how many accounts back it (log scale, capped). Never by amount, because of dust, and a free claim stays at the base size, hollow. Docs/17 proposed it; the current component refuses any size scale. Off switch: keep every dot 10px.
